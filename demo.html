Why Bhu Stack Transforms Municipal Governance

Bridging the gap between legal master plans, physical construction reality, and secondary property transactions.

For Municipalities & Town Planners

  • ✓ Autonomous Spatial Audits: Compare drone survey point clouds against sanctioned boundaries without manual site raids.
  • ✓ Automated Penalty Notices: Generate legal violation notices with exact spatial coordinates and excess height measurements.
  • ✓ Volumetric Strata Registry: Assign true 3D ULPIN codes to every vertical unit, parking allotment, and air rights.

For Homebuyers & Mortgage Banks

  • ✓ Zero-Fraud Verification: Scan a property's ULPIN QR code to verify if the 11th or 12th floor is legally approved before buying.
  • ✓ Bank Loan Due Diligence: Lenders instantly flag encumbered or unapproved floors, preventing non-performing bad mortgages.
  • ✓ Immutable History: An illegal floor constructed 3 years after handover can never be disguised as "original construction."
